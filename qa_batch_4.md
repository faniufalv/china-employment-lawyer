## Q1: [Financial Compliance] What is the statute of limitations for labor arbitration?

**A**: According to Article 27 of the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law, the limitation period is one year from when the party knew or should have known their rights were infringed. For wage disputes during ongoing employment, the one-year limit does not apply; but after termination, claims must be filed within one year.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国劳动法》

**领域**: 劳动法 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Financial Compliance] For Overseas Chinese: What are the new regulations on registered capital under the new Company Law?

**A**: The 2024 revised Company Law: 1) LLC shareholders must fully pay subscribed capital within 5 years from establishment; 2) Joint-stock company promoters must pay upon establishment; 3) State Council may set special provisions. Companies should evaluate capital payment plans promptly.

**法律依据**: 《公司登记管理条例》

**领域**: 新公司法 | **语言**: English
